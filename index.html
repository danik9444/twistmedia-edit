How do I send you the footage?
Google Drive, Dropbox, Frame.io, or any cloud storage you prefer. We'll adapt to your workflow — not the other way around.
What if I need revisions?
Our revision rate is under 5%, but when changes are needed, we turn them around within 48 hours. Every package includes at least 1 revision.
Do you really understand Jewish weddings?
We're based in Israel and have edited tens of thousands of Jewish weddings — Orthodox, Conservative, Reform, and everything in between. We know the chuppah, the hora, the sheva brachot, and every moment that matters. No brief needed.
What about time zones?
It actually works in your favor. Israel is 7 hours ahead of EST. You upload footage at night, and our team starts working on it first thing in the morning. You wake up to progress.
What's the turnaround during peak season?
14 days year-round. We have a full team — not a solo editor. Peak season doesn't slow us down because we scale capacity, not deadlines.
Is the first project really free?
Yes. No catch, no credit card, no commitment. We edit one wedding for you at no cost so you can see the quality firsthand. If you love it, we keep working together. If not, no hard feelings.
